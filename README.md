# Mental health and weather
Analysis of suicide rates in the United States, Spain and the Netherlands (2001–2021) and their relationship with weather, drug overdoses, population density and economic indicators.
All data files are in `datos/`. Sources: Open-Meteo (ERA5), CDC, NCHS, INE, U.S. Census Bureau, Eurostat and FRED.
The notebooks (`.ipynb`) contain the data processing and the plots; open them with Jupyter.
An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) was used to help write and debug the code; the analysis and its conclusions were reviewed by the team.
